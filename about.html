About

A delivery leader who now helps small businesses show up and run more clearly.

K&T Services is led by Terence Ngoh. He brings the same discipline he has used with enterprise teams — start with a clear picture, prioritize what matters, and improve in small steps — to local business owners.

Terence Ngoh

Terence helps small businesses get found, get chosen, and operate with less friction. That can mean building a website, cleaning up a Google Business Profile, tightening how inquiries are handled, or working through the operations issues that slow a team down.

For more than eight years he has worked as a Scrum Master and Agile delivery and program leader, helping teams plan work, remove blockers, and ship in short, useful cycles. He is a certified SAFe Agilist and has led delivery work for enterprise teams, including work with companies such as Costco, CAVA, U.N. Bank, and CVS Health.

He holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from the University of Buea.

He works with small businesses across the Washington DC, Maryland and Virginia area, and with owners remotely nationwide.

The approach

Clear, measurable, and iterative.

Audit first

We look at your current presence and process before recommending work. You should see the problem clearly before you invest in a fix.

Plain-English recommendations

You get advice you can understand and act on. No jargon for its own sake, and no bloated reports.

Measurable results

We define what “better” looks like up front — more complete listings, an easier way to book, fewer dropped inquiries — and check whether the work moved those things.

Agile, iterative delivery

Work happens in short cycles, with regular check-ins. That way you see progress early, and we can adjust as we learn.
