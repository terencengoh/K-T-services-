Services

Websites and local SEO for Washington DC, Maryland and Virginia.

K&T Services helps small businesses in the Washington DC, Maryland and Virginia area, and remotely nationwide, with website building, local visibility, and practical problem-solving. Each engagement starts with an audit so the work is aimed at the problems that actually cost you customers or time.

Website building

Your website should make it obvious what you offer, why someone should trust you, and what to do next — on a phone as easily as on a computer.

What’s included

  • A custom, mobile-responsive website with clear service or offering pages
  • A simple structure that helps visitors call, book, or inquire
  • Foundational search and accessibility practices (clear titles, headings, and page descriptions)
  • Contact paths, and online booking or inquiry forms when they are a good fit
  • Guidance on launching the site and keeping it accurate over time
Who it’s for Owners who have no website, an outdated site, or a site that looks fine but does not help visitors take the next step.

Local business growth

Most nearby customers in Washington DC, Maryland and Virginia decide whether to choose you long before they walk in. We help you show up clearly in search, on maps, and in the places people already check.

What’s included

  • Google Business Profile setup, cleanup, and ongoing completeness (photos, hours, services, and the right categories)
  • A practical plan for gathering and responding to reviews
  • Local SEO and consistent business information across key directories
  • Guidance on your social presence so it supports, rather than distracts from, real inquiries
  • Online booking or inquiry paths that connect your listings to an actual next step

Industry context, not a promise about your results: Google has reported that complete Business Profiles get 7x more clicks, and BrightLocal’s 2026 research found that 68% of consumers only use businesses with a 4-star rating or higher.

Who it’s for Shops, trades, clinics, restaurants, and other local businesses that depend on nearby customers finding them, trusting them, and making contact.

Small business problem-solving

Sometimes the website is not the real issue. The offer is unclear, intake is messy, follow-up falls through, or day-to-day work takes longer than it should. We help you find the leak and fix it.

What’s included

  • A focused review of how customers discover you, inquire, buy, and come back
  • Clearer offers, intake, and follow-up so fewer inquiries go cold
  • Practical operations improvements: handoffs, scheduling, and the steps that waste time
  • Recommendations in plain English, prioritized by impact and effort
  • Hands-on help implementing the changes, in short cycles you can see
Who it’s for Owners who know something is leaking sales or slowing the team, and want a practical fix — not a thick strategy deck.
